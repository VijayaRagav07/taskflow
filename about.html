A NOTE ABOUT TASKFLOW

SIMPLE TASKS. BETTER DAYS.

A calmer way to
get things done.

TaskFlow is a small, personal task manager built around one idea: a clear next step makes a busy day feel lighter.

Made to stay simple

Create a task, give it a category, and check it off when it is done. Your dashboard keeps a quick count, and the task list lets you search and filter whenever you need to find something.

Your tasks stay yours

TaskFlow saves your task list and theme preference in this browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing this browser’s site data also clears your saved tasks.
